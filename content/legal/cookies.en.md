tekomltd.com does not use cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

## What we do not use

- No cookies for visitor analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No embedded content from other sites (videos, maps, social media buttons) that could set its own cookies.
- No log-in, so no session cookies.

## What is stored in your browser

The only thing the site stores is your choice of light or dark theme, if you made one. It is kept in the browser's local storage, stays on your device and is never sent to us. It is not a cookie and holds no personal data. Delete it in your browser settings ("Clear site data") if you wish.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify processes the technical data of every request (such as the IP address) as described in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy), but sets no cookies on visitors of the site.
