tekomltd.com does not use cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

## What we do not use

- Cookies for visitor analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Embedded content from other sites (videos, maps, social media buttons) that sets its own cookies.
- Log-in to an account. So there are no session cookies either.

## What is stored in your browser

The site stores only one thing: your choice of light or dark theme, if you made one. The choice is kept in the browser's local storage. It stays only on your device and is never sent to us. It is not a cookie and holds no personal data. You can delete it in your browser settings ("Clear site data").

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify processes the technical data of every request (for example the IP address), as described in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy). Netlify sets no cookies on visitors of the site.
