The General Data Protection Regulation gives you the following rights over the personal data ТЕКОМ ООД (Tekom Ltd) processes about you. The site collects no data, so in practice these rights concern your correspondence with us and the hosting's technical records described in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Your rights

- **Access** – to learn whether we process data about you and to receive a copy.
- **Rectification** – to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
- **Erasure** – to have data deleted when it is no longer needed or the processing is unlawful, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- **Restriction** – to have processing paused while your request is checked.
- **Portability** – to receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection** – to object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

## How to exercise them

Write to [privacy@tekomltd.com](mailto:privacy@tekomltd.com) or by post to: ТЕКОМ ООД, 119 Hadzhi Dimitar Asenov St, Tekom Business Centre, 6000 Stara Zagora, Bulgaria.

- We answer within one month. For complex requests this can be extended by two further months; we will tell you if so.
- Exercising your rights is free of charge.
- We may ask you to confirm your identity before we disclose or delete data.

## Complaints

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd, 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria, [www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg). We would be glad if you contacted us first so we can resolve it together.
