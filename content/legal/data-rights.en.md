The General Data Protection Regulation gives you rights over your personal data that TEKOM Ltd processes. The site collects no data, so in practice these rights concern your correspondence with us and the hosting's technical records described in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Your rights

- **Access:** to learn whether we process data about you and to receive a copy of it.
- **Rectification:** to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure:** to ask us to delete the data when it is no longer needed or the processing is unlawful, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- **Restriction:** to ask us to pause processing while we check your request.
- **Portability:** to receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection:** to object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

## How to exercise them

Write to us at [privacy@tekomltd.com](mailto:privacy@tekomltd.com) or by post to: TEKOM Ltd (ТЕКОМ ООД), 119 Hadzhi Dimitar Asenov St, Tekom Business Centre, 6000 Stara Zagora, Bulgaria.

- We answer within one month. For complex requests this period can be extended by two further months. If so, we tell you.
- Exercising your rights is free of charge.
- We may ask you to confirm your identity before we disclose or delete data.

## Complaints

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd, 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria, [www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg). We ask you to contact us first, so we can resolve the matter together.
