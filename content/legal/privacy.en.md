Here we describe which personal data is processed when you visit tekomltd.com or contact us. This policy replaces the policy of the previous site. The new site has no comments, accounts, forms or cookies, so far less data is involved.

## Data controller

E-mail for questions about personal data: [privacy@tekomltd.com](mailto:privacy@tekomltd.com). Phone: +359 88 863 6034.

## What we do not do

- The site has no forms, log-in accounts, comments or newsletter. We do not collect data through the site.
- We do not use cookies, visitor analytics tools, advertising or tracking.
- Fonts, the map and the photos load from our own address. While you browse the site, it sends no requests to other sites (Google, Facebook and the like).

## Data when you visit the site

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). On every visit, Netlify's servers process technical data, as any web server does: IP address, date and time, the address of the requested page, browser type and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the page and to protect the site from abuse and attacks.

- Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
- Retention: Netlify keeps these records for a short period set out in its terms. After that it deletes them. We do not link them to individuals.
- Netlify is the only processor of personal data for the site. It acts on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Transfers of data to the USA rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Light or dark theme

If you choose the light or dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays only on your device and is not sent to us. It is not a cookie. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

## When you write to us or call

If you send us an e-mail or call us, we process the data you give us: name, e-mail address, phone and the content of your enquiry. We use it only to answer you and to prepare a contract proposal.

- Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (point (f)).
- Retention: we keep the correspondence as long as the enquiry needs it. If we sign a contract, we keep the documents for the periods the law requires (for example the Bulgarian Accountancy Act).
- We do not share this data with third parties unless the law requires it.

## Documents for EU programmes

Under "Publications" we publish documents for projects co-funded by the European Union. The programme rules require this. The documents contain company information. They contain no personal data of visitors.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection. We describe how to exercise your rights on the page [Your data rights](/en/data-rights).

## Changes

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and the date of the last change.
