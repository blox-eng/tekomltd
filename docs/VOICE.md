# Гласът на сайта / The site's voice

Every text on tekomltd.com follows these rules, in Bulgarian, English and German. `content:check` enforces the ones a machine can see: it refuses an em dash and the price, luxury and bargain words of rules 3 and 4 in all three languages (`apps/website/src/content/voice.ts`). It skips `eu-programmes.yaml`, `publications.yaml` and the invitations page, because their wording is official.

## The reader

Two readers at once:
- a person who can pay for anything and has seen every brochure;
- a person who counts every lev and fears a builder will cheat them.

Both trust the same thing: a calm voice that states facts. Neither trusts a slogan.

## Rules

1. **One fact beats one adjective.** Name the project, the year, the street, the m², the material, the system. "Калия, 2021: 1700 м² минерална вата" — not "качествена фасада".
2. **Never claim what the repo does not prove.** Use only facts already in the content. Do not invent numbers, awards, people or dates.
3. **No talk about price.** No "цена", "оферта", "бюджет", "количества", "договаряме", "евтино", "спестявате", "сметки", "инвестиция". We invite a visit (оглед) or a conversation, never a quote.
4. **No luxury words and no bargain words.** No "висок клас", "лукс", "премиум", "ексклузивен", "мечта", "уникален", "перфектен", "достъпен".
5. **No slogans.** A line that any builder in any country could say gets cut or gets a fact. "Нищо скрито", "Строим правилно", "Без изненади" are slogans.
6. **No "X, а не Y".** Say what we do. Do not set it against an imagined bad builder.
7. **No em dashes (—) in Bulgarian copy.** Use a comma, a colon or a new sentence. En dashes in ranges (9.00–18.00, 118–120) stay.
8. **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Active voice. Present tense for what we do, past tense for what we built.
9. **No jokes in captions.** Humour, if any, is one dry line in a section intro.
10. **Plain words.** "Строим", "полагаме", "предаваме". Not "реализираме", "осъществяваме", "предлагаме решения".
11. **"Вие" with a capital В** when we speak to the reader, as the site already does.
12. **Calls to action are statements.** "Изпратете ни адреса. Идваме на оглед." Not "Обмисляте ли…?"

## Legal and EU texts

Same voice, same clarity. Keep every legal meaning, every right, every deadline, every contact and every programme or contract number exactly. The EU publicity sentences that the funding contract requires stay word for word.
